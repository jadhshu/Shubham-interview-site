Problem Statement

The Operational Bottleneck

Manual activation of BLE sensor tags created delays, inconsistencies, and quality issues across multiple global locations.

Activation took hours; often during breaks
Processes differed across WH, TSC, Hilti Stores, 3PLs
Early activation → battery drain → complaints
Activation delays slowed packing & shipping

Why It Was Hard

  • • Multi‑team, multi‑location workflow
  • • No standard process
  • • High‑volume fleet customers
  • • Manual steps → human error + inefficiency

Why It Mattered

  • • Customer quality issues
  • • Wasted labor hours
  • • Electronic waste
  • • Delivery delays
